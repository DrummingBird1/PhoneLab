PhoneLab shows what your phone's hardware is doing right now: every sensor, temperature and system stat, live, in one app.

<b>Sensors</b>
• Live readings from up to 24 hardware sensors: accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, gravity, rotation vectors, barometer, light, proximity, humidity, ambient temperature, heart rate, step counter, hinge angle and more
• GPS speed and altitude, plus a sound-level meter
• Textual cards with full precision, or a visual mode with gauges and a live compass
• Record a session to CSV; recording keeps running in the background

<b>System and hardware</b>
• 8 thermal zones (CPU, GPU, battery, skin, modem, Wi-Fi, charger, NPU) with a CPU temperature trend
• Battery health: status, charging power in watts, estimated capacity, charge cycles and a 24-hour level history
• Per-core CPU frequencies, display refresh rate and HDR, hardware capabilities
• Device specs including the GPU, with text or JSON export
• A 4-phase CPU benchmark

<b>Tools</b>
• Bubble level
• Compass calibration
• Magnetometer metal detector
• Vibration seismograph with frequency analysis

<b>Device check</b>
Buying or selling a used phone? Run 11 guided tests (touch screen, multi-touch, display, speaker, microphone, vibration, volume buttons, proximity, flashlight, motion sensors and battery) and share a pass/fail report.

<b>Alerts and extras</b>
• Custom alerts when a reading crosses your threshold, and CPU temperature alerts
• Home-screen widget and Quick Settings tile for CPU temperature
• Material 3 design, dark and light themes, optional Material You colors
• English, Hebrew, Spanish and Arabic; metric or imperial units

<b>Privacy</b>
No ads, no account, no tracking. PhoneLab has no internet permission, so your sensor data never leaves your device. Permissions are requested only when you tap a feature that needs them.

<b>Free and open source</b>
Every feature is free, with no in-app purchases. PhoneLab is open source under the GPL-3.0 license.

Sensors available vary by device; the app clearly marks what your hardware doesn't have.
