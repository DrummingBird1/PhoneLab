Every sensor on your phone.
Live, in one screen.

PhoneLab surfaces every hardware sensor, system stat, and thermal zone your Android device has — in real time, with no ads, no account, and no network access.

⬇ Download the APK View source on GitHub
Free · Open source · Works fully offline

What it shows you

Five tabs, one continuous live view of your device's hardware.

📡

~29 sensors

Accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, gravity, barometer, light, proximity, humidity, heart rate, step counter, and more.

🌡️

Thermal zones

CPU, GPU, battery, and skin temperature with color-coded, hysteresis-protected alerts.

🏁

Performance benchmark

A 4-phase test — integer, floating-point, memory, and multi-core — with a plain-language rating.

🔧

Hardware tab

Per-core CPU frequency, detailed battery stats, display capabilities, and biometric/NFC/Bluetooth flags.

📝

CSV recording

Record a live sensor session to CSV in the background via a foreground service, then share the file.

🏠

Widget & QS tile

Check CPU temperature from your home screen or Quick Settings without opening the app.

🎨

Textual or Visual

Raw numbers for developers, or icons and gauges for a glance — switch instantly.

🌐

4 languages

English, Hebrew, Spanish, and Arabic, with full RTL support and dark/light themes.

See it in action

All five tabs, straight from a real device.

🌐 Don't want to install anything?

PhoneLab Web mirrors the sensor dashboard right in your browser — motion, orientation, GPS, ambient light, an internet speed test, and CSV/image export. Nothing installed, nothing sent anywhere.
