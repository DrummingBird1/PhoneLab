Every sensor on your phone.
Live, in one app.

PhoneLab shows every hardware sensor, temperature and system stat your Android phone has, in real time, plus battery health, sensor tools and a full device check. No ads, no account, and your readings stay on your device.

Free · Open source (GPL-3.0) · Android 8.0+ · English, עברית, Español, العربية

What it shows you

Seven tabs: Sensors, System, Hardware, Tools, Device check, About and Settings, all reading your hardware live.

📡

24 sensors, live

Accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, gravity, rotation vectors, barometer, light, proximity, humidity, heart rate, step counter and more, plus GPS speed and a sound-level meter.

🌡️

8 thermal zones

CPU, GPU, battery, skin, modem, Wi-Fi, charger and NPU temperatures, with a CPU temperature trend.

🔋

Battery health

Health status, charging power in watts, estimated capacity, charge cycles and a 24-hour level history.

🔧

Hardware details

Per-core CPU frequencies, display refresh rate and HDR, NFC, Bluetooth, camera and biometric capabilities.

🧰

Sensor tools

A bubble level, compass calibration, a magnetometer metal detector and a vibration seismograph with frequency analysis.

✅

Device check

Buying or selling a used phone? Run 11 guided hardware tests and share a pass/fail report.

🏁

CPU benchmark

A 4-phase test (integer, floating-point, memory and multi-core) with a plain-language rating.

🔔

Custom alerts

"Notify me when X goes above Y" for any reading, plus CPU temperature alerts that won't spam you.

📝

Record & export

Record sensors to CSV in the background, and export your device specs as text or JSON.

🏠

Widget & Quick Settings tile

Check CPU temperature from your home screen or Quick Settings without opening the app.

🎨

Material 3

Textual cards for exact numbers or gauges for a glance, dark and light themes, and optional Material You colors.

🌐

4 languages

English, Hebrew, Spanish and Arabic with full right-to-left support, in metric or imperial units.
