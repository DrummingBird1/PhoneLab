PhoneLab

Privacy Policy

Effective & last updated: August 16, 2026

In one paragraph

PhoneLab has no internet access at all — the app does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so it is technically incapable of sending anything to anyone, ever. No servers, no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Every sensor reading is calculated and shown on your screen only. The only files PhoneLab ever creates are the ones you ask for — a CSV or JSON export — and they stay on your device until you choose to share them yourself.

Who publishes PhoneLab

PhoneLab (published on Google Play under the package id com.sensolab.devicemonitor) is developed by an independent developer. This policy explains what the app does and doesn't do with data on your device, since PhoneLab is a hardware-monitoring tool that necessarily reads from sensors, location, and audio input to do its job.

Data we collect

None. PhoneLab does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data, because it has no way to send data anywhere — the app is built without the INTERNET permission and contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting software development kit of any kind. Every dependency the app ships with is a standard Android UI library; none of them talk to a network.

Everything described below happens locally, inside your device's memory or storage, using Android's own sensor and file APIs.

PhoneLab asks for each permission only when you open the screen that needs it — never all at once at launch — and only uses it for the specific feature listed here.

Files you create

Two features write files, and both are actions you start yourself:

  • Sensor recording — tap Record on the Sensors tab to save a stream of sensor readings to a .csv file.
  • Spec export — tap Export on the System tab to save your device's specifications as a .txt or .json file.

These files are saved to your device's own storage (your Documents folder, or the app's private cache as a fallback). PhoneLab never uploads them anywhere. They leave your device only if you explicitly use the share sheet to send, email, or copy one yourself.

No accounts, no ads, no analytics

PhoneLab has no sign-in, no user accounts, and stores no profile about you anywhere. It shows no advertising and includes no third-party advertising or analytics libraries. There is nothing to opt out of, because nothing is being measured or shown to you from outside the app.

Children's privacy

PhoneLab is rated for Everyone and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, for the simple reason described above: it collects no information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of PhoneLab adds a feature that does use the network — the "last updated" date at the top of this page will change, and the new policy will describe exactly what changed and why.
